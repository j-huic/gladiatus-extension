Guild Market Helper

Suggest a price for staged Mini-Pumpkin stacks using your chosen per-item value.

Mini-Pumpkin pricing

Shows the stack calculation beside the Guild Market price field.

Use a positive whole number. The default is 100,000 gold.

What it does

After the game stages a matching item, the helper displays the quantity × unit price calculation. Apply fills only the price field and recalculates the fee. It never lists the item for you.
